# Data Breach Notification Procedures

## Overview

This document outlines the procedures for detecting, assessing, and notifying data breaches in compliance with GDPR Article 33 and 34, and Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

## Legal Requirements

### GDPR Article 33 - Notification to Supervisory Authority

- **72-hour requirement:** Notify Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach
- **Content:** Description of breach, categories of data, number of individuals, likely consequences, measures taken
- **Delay:** If notification cannot be made within 72 hours, provide reasons for delay

### GDPR Article 34 - Communication to Data Subjects

- **Without undue delay:** Notify affected individuals if breach is likely to result in high risk to rights and freedoms
- **Content:** Nature of breach, contact details of DPO, likely consequences, measures taken
- **Exemptions:** Not required if:
  - Data is encrypted
  - Measures have been taken to mitigate risk
  - Notification would involve disproportionate effort

## Breach Detection

### Sources of Breach Detection

1. **Automated Monitoring:**
   - Security system alerts
   - Unusual access patterns
   - Failed authentication attempts
   - Network intrusion detection

2. **Manual Detection:**
   - User reports
   - Staff reports
   - Third-party notifications
   - Security audits

3. **Regular Reviews:**
   - Access logs
   - System audits
   - Security assessments

## Breach Assessment

### Immediate Actions (Within 1 Hour)

1. **Contain the Breach:**
   - Isolate affected systems
   - Revoke compromised credentials
   - Disable affected accounts
   - Block unauthorized access

2. **Document Initial Details:**
   - Date and time of discovery
   - Who discovered the breach
   - Initial assessment of scope
   - Immediate containment measures

3. **Notify Internal Team:**
   - Data Protection Officer (if appointed)
   - IT Security Team
   - Management
   - Legal/Compliance Team

### Assessment Criteria (Within 24 Hours)

Assess the breach for:

1. **Nature of Breach:**
   - What happened?
   - How did it occur?
   - What systems/data are affected?

2. **Categories of Data:**
   - Personal data
   - Special category data (health data)
   - Financial data
   - Authentication data

3. **Number of Individuals Affected:**
   - Approximate count
   - Categories of individuals

4. **Likely Consequences:**
   - Identity theft risk
   - Financial loss risk
   - Reputation damage
   - Discrimination risk
   - Other significant harm

5. **Risk Level:**
   - Low: Minimal risk to individuals
   - Medium: Some risk, but mitigatable
   - High: Significant risk to rights and freedoms

## Breach Notification Process

### Step 1: Internal Notification (Immediate)

**Who to Notify:**

- Data Protection Officer (if appointed)
- IT Security Team
- Management
- Legal/Compliance Team

**Information to Provide:**

- Brief description of breach
- Affected systems/data
- Initial containment measures
- Next steps

### Step 2: DPC Notification (Within 72 Hours)

**When to Notify:**

- All breaches that pose a risk to individuals
- Even if full details are not yet available

**How to Notify:**

- Online: https://www.dataprotection.ie
- Email: info@dataprotection.ie
- Phone: +353 57 868 4800

**Required Information:**

1. Description of the nature of the breach
2. Categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned
3. Categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned
4. Name and contact details of the data protection officer (if applicable)
5. Description of the likely consequences of the breach
6. Description of measures taken or proposed to address the breach

**Template for DPC Notification:**

```
Subject: Data Breach Notification - [Date]

Dear Data Protection Commission,

We are notifying you of a personal data breach in accordance with Article 33 of GDPR.

1. Nature of Breach: [Description]
2. Date and Time of Breach: [Date/Time]
3. Date and Time of Discovery: [Date/Time]
4. Categories of Data: [List]
5. Number of Data Subjects Affected: [Approximate number]
6. Categories of Data Subjects: [List]
7. Likely Consequences: [Description]
8. Measures Taken: [Description]
9. Measures Proposed: [Description]
10. Contact Details: [DPO/Contact Person]

We will provide further updates as the investigation progresses.

Yours sincerely,
[Name]
[Title]
[Contact Information]
```

### Step 3: Individual Notification (Without Undue Delay)

**When to Notify:**

- If breach is likely to result in high risk to rights and freedoms
- Unless data is encrypted or measures have been taken to mitigate risk

**How to Notify:**

- Email (preferred)
- Postal mail (if no email)
- Public notice (if individual notification not feasible)

**Required Information:**

1. Description of the nature of the breach
2. Name and contact details of the data protection officer (if applicable)
3. Description of the likely consequences of the breach
4. Description of measures taken or proposed to address the breach

**Template for Individual Notification:**

```
Subject: Important: Data Security Incident Notification

Dear [Name],

We are writing to inform you of a data security incident that may have affected your personal data.

What Happened:
[Description of breach]

What Information Was Involved:
[Categories of data]

What We Are Doing:
[Measures taken and proposed]

What You Can Do:
[Recommendations for individuals]

For More Information:
[Contact details]

We sincerely apologize for this incident and any concern it may cause.

Yours sincerely,
[Name]
[Title]
[Contact Information]
```

## Breach Response Team

### Roles and Responsibilities

1. **Incident Response Lead:**
   - Coordinate response
   - Make notification decisions
   - Communicate with stakeholders

2. **IT Security Team:**
   - Contain breach
   - Investigate cause
   - Implement fixes
   - Assess technical impact

3. **Data Protection Officer:**
   - Assess legal implications
   - Coordinate notifications
   - Document breach
   - Liaise with DPC

4. **Legal/Compliance Team:**
   - Review legal obligations
   - Prepare notifications
   - Handle regulatory communications

5. **Communications Team:**
   - Prepare public statements (if needed)
   - Handle media inquiries
   - Manage customer communications

## Breach Documentation

### Breach Register

Maintain a register of all breaches containing:

1. Date and time of breach
2. Date and time of discovery
3. Description of breach
4. Categories of data affected
5. Number of individuals affected
6. Risk assessment
7. Notification details (DPC and individuals)
8. Measures taken
9. Outcome
10. Lessons learned

### Retention

- Retain breach records for minimum of 3 years
- Review regularly for patterns and improvements

## Post-Breach Actions

### Immediate (Within 1 Week)

1. Complete investigation
2. Implement additional security measures
3. Notify all required parties
4. Document all actions

### Short-term (Within 1 Month)

1. Review and update security measures
2. Conduct security audit
3. Review access controls
4. Update incident response procedures

### Long-term (Within 3 Months)

1. Complete security improvements
2. Staff training on lessons learned
3. Review and update policies
4. Regular security assessments

## Prevention Measures

### Regular Security Measures

1. **Access Controls:**
   - Role-based access
   - Regular access reviews
   - Strong authentication
   - Principle of least privilege

2. **Monitoring:**
   - Security system monitoring
   - Access log reviews
   - Anomaly detection
   - Regular security audits

3. **Encryption:**
   - Data encryption at rest
   - Data encryption in transit
   - Backup encryption

4. **Staff Training:**
   - Security awareness training
   - Phishing prevention
   - Data handling procedures
   - Incident reporting procedures
